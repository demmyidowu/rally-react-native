RallyRide

Safe rides, organized by your chapter.

RallyRide is a designated driver coordination app for college Greek organizations. During chapter events, members can request safe rides home and get matched with volunteer designated drivers in real-time. Admins manage events, assign DDs, and monitor ride activity — all from one app.
