# Contact Us

## RallyRide

We're here to help. If you have questions, feedback, or need assistance with the RallyRide app, please reach out using any of the methods below.

---

## Support

**Email**: support@rallyride.app
**Response Time**: Within 48 hours

For the fastest response, please include:
- Your account email address
- A description of your issue or question
- Screenshots if applicable
- Your device type (iPhone or Android)

---

## Report a Bug

Found something not working correctly? Email us at **support@rallyride.app** with the subject line "Bug Report" and include:
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Your device model and operating system version

---

## Privacy Concerns

For questions about your data or to request account deletion, email **support@rallyride.app** with the subject line "Privacy Request." See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for full details on how we handle your information.
