Contact Us

We're here to help. Reach out with questions, feedback, or if you need assistance with the RallyRide app.

Support

Email: support@rallyride.app

Response Time: Within 48 hours

For the fastest response, please include:

  • Your account email address
  • A description of your issue or question
  • Screenshots if applicable
  • Your device type (iPhone or Android)

Report a Bug

Found something not working correctly? Email us at support@rallyride.app with the subject line "Bug Report" and include:

  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Your device model and OS version

Privacy Concerns

For questions about your data or to request account deletion, email support@rallyride.app with the subject line "Privacy Request." See our Privacy Policy for full details on how we handle your information.
